## Integration

- ROLES: `.roles/ROLE.md` covers kernel-boundary stewardship, manifest-contract
  audit, and consumer-migration review.
- VTRACE: RLINE does not currently carry a repo-local VTRACE matrix; PITFALL
  entries cite specs, compatibility policy, wave docs, roles, and executable
  validation until a trace slice exists.
- Tests: workspace tests, RHIST/RCTX fixture verification, CLI smokes, and
  RCOUNT downstream rehearsal are the evidence hooks for PITFALL entries.
